Uprising - Performing Arts Development

An immersive program designed for aspiring youth musicians ready to elevate their craft and make their mark in the industry. Participants will refine essential skills like stage etiquette, public relations, and audience engagement while receiving professional feedback from experienced mentors. Beyond skill-building, Uprising offers exclusive access to some of Las Vegas' premier concert venues, providing hands-on opportunities to perform in world-class settings. This transformative experience empowers young artists to grow creatively, professionally, and personally, preparing them for the dynamic world of live performance.

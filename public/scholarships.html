The Dream Catcher Scholarship

At Manifest Academy, we believe everyone deserves the chance to pursue their dreams. Our mission is to provide equal access to arts education, regardless of financial circumstances. We are proud to have awarded over $75,000 in scholarships to more than 300 local youth, ensuring that the underserved have as much access as possible to resources we can provide.

Our scholarships can cover the cost of any of our available programs, performance opportunities, artist development and more. Manifest Academy is more than an educational organization — it's a creative community. We are committed to nurturing a lasting network of young artists and professionals, empowering them to grow and collaborate for years to come. ​
​
​To apply for a scholarship opportunity, please complete the application form found via the link below.

Requirements to apply are as follows:

  1. Video from applicant that tells us what your aspirations are as an artist. What are your goals? What inspires you? What does music mean to you?
  2. Hardship letter from parent or guardian in Word document or PDF detailing financial hardship. (Scholarship requests above 40% of program fees may require additional hardship documentation).


All submissions are reviewed and approved upon a case-by-case basis. Partial or Full Scholarships are at the discretion of Manifest Academy Las Vegas, Inc. and offered as availability allows. Please send your submission to manifestacademyinfo@gmail.com

Our Mission

"Manifest Academy is a 501 (c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to enabling all youth musicians to reach their full potential as productive, educated professionals. We provide artist development, educational resources, industry resources, and music mentorship for underserved youth. Students aged 6-18 receive hands-on training in music theory, recording arts, mental health development, and entrepreneurship. We achieve our objectives by partnering with schools, recreation centers, juvenile correctional facilities, and instructor led online courses."

Explore Our Programs

Manifest Academy offers 5 academic programs to enrich our students' futures. Youth benefit from expert faculty, an innovative curriculum, and the resources of the Las Vegas music community.

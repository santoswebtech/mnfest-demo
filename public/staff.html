Rob Lacy

Founder | CEO

Robert Lacy is a dynamic and dedicated youth advocate with a passion for empowering young musicians in Las Vegas, NV. With over 20 years of experience as teacher, mentor, music producer, event planner, he has become a driving force in creating opportunities for youth in the community to explore their musical talents and pursue their dreams.

As a music educator, Robert is committed to nurturing the next generation of musicians through mentorship and education programs. He dedicates his time to teach music classes, workshops, and seminars, empowering youth with the skills and knowledge they need to succeed in the industry. Through his mentorship, many of his students have gone on to pursue careers in music and continue to make meaningful contributions to their communities.

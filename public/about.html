OUR
STORY

A Professional Creative Arts Pathway for Youth

Manifest Academy delivers a structured, cohort-based program that guides youth from skill development to rehearsal to the stage. Through MAAD, participants create original work, build confidence, and gain real performance experience in professional studios and event spaces. Programming is offered at no cost to participants, with cohorts scheduled based on program capacity.

HOW MAAD
WORKS
PHASE 1 CREATE Instruction in songwriting, recording, performance, business of music
PHASE 2 PRACTICE Rehearsal + Mentor feedback aligned with professional standards
PHASE 3 PERFORM Public showcases + Publishing
WHY IT
MATTERS
A generation of young people are searching for guidance, identity, and purpose. Without supportive mentorship and healthy outlets, many are pulled toward negative influences. MAAD provides a safe, structured space where youth build confidence and self empowerment through art, music, and performance. By removing economic barriers, MAAD creates access to professional quality music education and high level creative experiences that many families could not otherwise afford.
OUR
IMPACT
Across the Las Vegas Valley, including juvenile detention centers, recreation centers, and schools, Manifest Academy delivers MAAD to give youth a healthy outlet from daily pressure and stress. Through the Pengame curriculum and artist development coaching, participants turn lived experience into original music and performance that supports emotional wellness, confidence, and self expression. We also build real workforce skills in music, including writing, recording, rehearsal, and professional stage readiness, so youth leave with discipline, teamwork, and tools they can use long after the program ends.

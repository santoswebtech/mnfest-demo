IMPACT
A healthy outlet. A stronger future

We believe every young artist deserves access to professional creative arts instruction, mentorship, and real performance opportunities. Through Manifest Academy Artist Development (MAAD), we provide youth ages 8–19 with a structured pathway from recording arts to publishing to the stage at no cost to participants, with cohorts offered based on program capacity.

WHERE
WE SERVE

Across the Las Vegas Valley, Manifest Academy serves youth through partnerships and programming in juvenile detention centers, recreation centers, schools, and community venues. We meet young people where they are and provide a consistent, structured space for mentorship, artistic discovery, and healthy expression. By bringing MAAD into the places youth already trust and access, we expand opportunity beyond traditional studios and stages. This creates a clear pathway from learning and rehearsal to publishing readiness and public performance.

WHAT WE
PROVIDE
  • Professional music production support and mentorship
  • Self publishing and the business of music education
  • Workforce development through discipline, collaboration, and professional expectations
  • Studio and rehearsal exposure that builds real readiness
  • Public performance opportunities with structured preparation

Things to Know - Business of Music Program

An introductory course designed to equip aspiring artists, producers, and music professionals with the essential tools and knowledge to navigate the business side of the music industry. Students will explore key topics such as artist management, music marketing, copyright and publishing, revenue streams, branding, and networking strategies. With a focus on real-world applications, the course combines lectures, case studies, and hands-on projects to teach how to build sustainable careers in the ever- evolving music landscape. Whether you're an emerging artist or an entrepreneur with big industry dreams, this course provides the foundation to thrive in the business of music.
